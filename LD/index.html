LD CMQ Score Rules

The score uses turn amplitude only. Time, discontinuities, reverses, deviation area and P95 deviation are calculated and stored for later comparison with clinician ratings. Forward-and-back tremor cannot be measured on a flat screen, so lifts are recorded as discontinuities.
