Clinical Reference

SARA + Tremor Weighting

Dysmetria = distance from target when the cursor first stops moving (arrival point). Tremor = the 95th-percentile oscillation radius around the median held position during the 2 seconds after arrival. Original SARA Finger Chase uses dysmetria only. Drag the bar to control how much the optional CeMoQu tremor measure contributes (0% excludes it; snaps to 10%).
