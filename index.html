CeMoQu: Cerebellar Motor Quantification

Four browser-based tasks, no specialized hardware — each produces a continuous, quantitative trace that supplements (not replaces) clinical rating scales.

Speech Disturbance Test

Reads guided passages, repeats "pa-ta-ka" rapidly, and sustains an "Ah" vowel. Extracts voice duration, loudness stability, pause ratio, and pitch (F0) variation to quantify dysarthric speech changes over time.

Microphone Open

Random Target Touch Test

Reach and tap targets that appear at random screen positions using mouse, touch, or webcam-tracked movement. Captures reaction time, final positional error, tremor, and time spent within the target zone.

Mouse · Touch · Camera Open

Line Drawing Test

Traces horizontal, vertical, and diagonal guide lines with the mouse or a touchscreen. Measures completion time, path deviation from the guide, discontinuities, corrective turns, and time spent outside the drawing bounds.

Mouse · Touch Open

Sitting Balance Test

Sits facing a webcam while pose tracking follows shoulder position over time. Measures upper-body sway and how level the shoulders stay, without any wearable sensors.

Webcam Open
